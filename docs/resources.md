## Calibration advice from passers

- People who passed were scoring **~85% on practice tests** before sitting the real thing. Aim well above the line on mocks.
- Multi-select ("choose two/three") questions are the trickiest format — use spare time to re-check those.
- Avoid exam dumps: the pool rotates, and they don't work. Community practice tests + the official assessment are the legitimate equivalents.
- If you studied in English, take the exam in English — translations can mismatch study-material terminology.
